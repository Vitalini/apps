Audinio Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Audinio is an Android podcast and local audio player developed by Vitalii Borovyk. Contact: 8244824@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Audinio stores subscriptions, queue entries, settings, playback positions, listening history, achievements, and downloaded audio in app storage. Imported audio files are copied into app storage. Audinio does not upload these audio files to its developer.

Search, feeds, and playback

Podcast search sends your search terms to the Apple iTunes directory. Audinio requests feeds, artwork, and audio from publishers and hosting providers. These services receive your IP address and request details. Their privacy policies govern their processing and retention of this information.

Audinio has no developer-operated account server, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK in version 0.4.5. Podcast audio can contain publisher advertisements.

Optional Google Drive synchronization

If you connect your Google account, Audinio stores your account email locally and requests access to its private Google Drive app-data folder. It does not request general access to your Drive files.

Synchronization sends subscriptions, settings, queue and playback state, history, achievements, and imported-audio metadata to your Google Drive. It does not include audio files. Google processes authentication and storage requests under its own privacy policy.

Synchronization restores saved state when you connect and can run periodically with an internet connection. Android scheduling and network conditions can delay it. Google Drive access may be limited to approved test accounts during testing.

Manual backups

You can export metadata backups to a location you choose. Audinio does not encrypt these exported JSON files. Protect them and share them only with people you trust.

Permissions

Audinio uses network access for search, feeds, downloads, and optional synchronization. A media playback foreground service supports background audio. Notification permission supports playback controls. The Android document picker provides access to files you select for import or export.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it or clear Android app storage. Uninstalling Audinio removes its private local storage. Removing an imported audio file from Audinio does not remove your original file.

Disconnecting Google Drive stops future synchronization but preserves existing cloud snapshots. To remove them, use Google Drive settings, Manage apps, Audinio, and delete hidden app data. You can also revoke Google account access.

Uninstalling Audinio does not remove Google Drive snapshots or exported backups. Remove exported files from their saved location separately. Deletion of a backup does not remove copies on other devices.

Children

Audinio is not designed specifically for children. Public podcast feeds can contain mature content. Audinio does not provide a child-safe content guarantee.
