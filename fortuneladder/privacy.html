Fortune Ladder Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Fortune Ladder is an Android life-simulation game developed by Vitalii Borovyk. Contact: 8244824@gmail.com.

Data collection

Fortune Ladder does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or device identifiers. The app:

  • has no internet access permission and makes no network requests;
  • has no analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs;
  • has no account system, sign-in, or user profile;
  • does not use in-app purchases or any payment system;
  • does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other device permission.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — current level, money, stats, and choices — is saved locally on your device using Android's DataStore Preferences. This data never leaves your device and is not visible to the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

Android's standard device backup (Google's OS-level "Back up my data" feature, if enabled on your device) may include this local save file as part of your own Google Account backup, the same way it backs up other apps' local data. This is a feature of the Android operating system, not something Fortune Ladder initiates or has access to — the developer never receives a copy of this data.

Children's privacy

Fortune Ladder does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync — this page will be updated and the "Effective date" above will change accordingly.
