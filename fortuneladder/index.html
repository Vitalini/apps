Fortune Ladder

Offline life and money simulator. Climb 300 levels from broke to fortune.

A free mobile life-sim climb for Android by Vitalii Borovyk. No ads, no accounts, no data collection — just strategic choices and consequence.

Features

  • Weekly turns: plan a limited set of actions each in-game week and live with the outcome
  • Economic ladder: jobs, stocks, businesses, hiring
  • 300 levels of progression from first paycheck to running an empire
  • Five branching paths: career, founder, investor, family, grey zone — each with different risks and rewards
  • Businesses and investments can fail: manage risk, not guaranteed wins
  • Fully offline with no ads, no in-app purchases, no account required
  • Progress saved on your device only
  • Available in English and Ukrainian
